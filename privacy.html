Privacy Policy

Alumni Atlas · Last updated 27 October 2026

Alumni Atlas is a desktop app that connects college students and alumni. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with LinkedIn or Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and whether your email is verified. We do not receive your LinkedIn or Google password.
  • Profile details you add. Such as your college, branch, batch, employer, job title, headline and LinkedIn profile link.
  • Location. The city or area you choose for your profile, used to place you on the alumni map. Device location is used only if you allow it.
  • Community activity. Communities you join or create, messages and posts you send, event RSVPs, poll votes, meetings and connection requests.
  • Settings. Your privacy and visibility choices.

How we use it

  • To create your account and keep one account per email address.
  • To show your profile to other members, according to your visibility settings.
  • To run communities, messaging, events and the alumni map.
  • To answer questions you ask the in-app assistant.
  • To keep the service safe, including handling reports and blocking abuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, Alumni Atlas only requests your basic profile (name, email address and profile photo) through the openid, email and profile scopes. This data is used only to sign you in and to fill in your Alumni Atlas profile. It is not shared with third parties except the service providers listed below, and it is not used for advertising. Alumni Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores account and app data and handles sign-in.
  • LinkedIn and Google confirm your identity when you sign in.
  • AI and map services process assistant questions and turn place names into map positions.
  • Email delivery sends invitations and event updates you ask for.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and visibility settings in the app at any time.
  • You can leave communities and block other members.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by opening a request on the contact page below.

Data retention and security

We keep your data while your account is active. When you ask for deletion, we remove your account and profile, except where we must keep limited records for safety or legal reasons. Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so members only see what they are allowed to see.

Children

Alumni Atlas is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/SHERVINdias/alumni-atlas/issues
